Everything You Need To Know

AutoMileage uses Android's native Activity Recognition Transitions API. When your phone detects stationary pedestrian movement, GPS hardware remains asleep. Only when vehicular transit is detected does the high-accuracy fused location provider engage. This results in less than 1.8% battery usage across an entire 8-hour shift.

Yes. The IRS standard for deductible business vehicle use (Treasury Reg. § 1.274-5T) requires timely recordkeeping of: (1) date, (2) mileage, (3) destination/location, and (4) business purpose. AutoMileage records every required field and exports complete CSV records formatted specifically for Schedule C filings.

We built AutoMileage with an offline-first architecture. Because your data is stored locally in your phone's encrypted Room SQLite database rather than on costly cloud servers, we do not have huge recurring cloud overhead to pass onto you. You own your data—no accounts, no passwords, no subscriptions.
