Privacy Policy for AutoMileage: 1099 Tax Tracker

Last Updated: September 25, 2026 • Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection & Absolute Privacy

At Software Company LLC, we believe your driving routes, locations, and financial data belong to you alone. AutoMileage is built with a strict offline-first architecture:

  • We do not operate user telemetry or tracking servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • We embed zero commercial tracking SDKs, advertising frameworks, or behavior analytics tools.
  • No user account, email address, phone number, or password is ever required to operate the application.

2. Data Handled Strictly On Your Local Device

All application records are stored exclusively in your device's private SQLite Room database (mileagetracker.db):

  • Location Data (GPS Waypoints): AutoMileage requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION solely to detect routes, measure driving distances, and calculate standard mileage deductions when vehicular movement is detected.
  • Physical Activity: AutoMileage utilizes ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION transitions to detect vehicular transit and enter stationary low-power sleep modes to conserve battery.
  • Financial & Expense Data: Amounts, receipt dates, vendor names, and vehicle profiles are retained strictly on-device.

3. Background Location & Foreground Service

To safely record trips without requiring driver distraction, AutoMileage operates an Android Foreground Service with type location accompanied by a persistent notification in your status bar. Background GPS tracking runs only while active vehicular transit is occurring.

4. User Data Exports & Deletion

You may export your data at any time as an IRS Schedule C CSV report or encrypted backup file. You can delete individual items or completely wipe all local data via Settings > Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the application.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance team at privacy@softwarecompany.example.com.
